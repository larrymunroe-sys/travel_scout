📖 Travel Scout • User Guide & How-To

🌍 Welcome to Travel Scout!

A standalone collaborative platform designed for multi-city journeys with date-matched accommodations, team co-planning, and real-time cultural discovery.

1. Trip Management & Customization

  • ✏️ Customize: Click to rename your trip or update its description/purpose anytime.
  • ➕ New Trip: Create a brand-new trip from scratch (e.g. Japan, Italy, California) with your first city, dates, and starting hotel.
  • Trip Switcher: Use the dropdown in the header to jump between saved itineraries.

2. Cities & Accommodations Manager

  • ➕ Add Destination City: Add any city worldwide with arrival/departure dates and initial hotel.
  • 🗑️ Delete City: Remove unwanted cities with automatic timeline re-indexing.
  • 🏨 Add Hotel by Date: Moving hotels midway through a city stay? Add secondary hotels with specific check-in/out dates. Transit calculations automatically switch to whichever hotel is active on each day!

3. Collaborative Itinerary & Timeline

  • 📋 Bucket List (To-Do): Hold unscheduled activities until you decide which day to visit them.
  • 📅 Date Assignment: Use the date dropdown on any card to move it to a specific day or return to the Bucket List.
  • 👤 Author Attribution: Every card shows the creator's avatar badge so you always know who suggested what.
  • 🗑️ Remove Stops: Click the red trash icon on any card to delete it.

4. Interactive Links & Directions

  • Clickable Title (↗): Click any card title to open its official homepage or primary review in a new tab.
  • 🌐 [Website / Source] ↗: Blue link pill directly accessing the venue's web page or guide.
  • 📍 Maps & Directions ↗: Amber link pill opening Google Maps navigation from your active hotel.

5. Autonomous Cultural Scout & Daily Scanner

  • City-Scoped Search: Search guides, blogs, Reddit, or TikTok trends for specific cities.
  • ⚡ Daily Autonomous Scan: Click the glowing scan button to analyze all trip cities at once and import new festivals, concerts, and pop-ups directly to your wishlist.

6. Multi-User Collaboration

  • 👥 Share & Invite: Invite friends by email with Editor or Viewer roles.
  • Switch User Dropdown: Switch accounts or add new traveler profiles on the fly to simulate real-time teamwork.
